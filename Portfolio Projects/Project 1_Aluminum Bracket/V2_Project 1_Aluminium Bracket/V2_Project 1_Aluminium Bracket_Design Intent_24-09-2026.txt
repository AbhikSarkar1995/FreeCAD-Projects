1. Length, width, height of the bracket are as per client criterion to fit inside a box of 120 X 80 X 60 mm.
2. Base length and width are selected as 120 X 60 mm for maximum attachment stability with the connecting parts.
3. Hole diameters are selected with a little extra tolerance to accommodate the ISO M8 size bolts with enough clearance.
4. Base hole positions are selected to maximize the base anchoring, vertical hole positions based on the symmetry considerations. 
5. All holes have ISO M8 countersink for flus fitting of star, plus , minus, allen or torx type countersink bolt heads.
6. Corner radius of the bracket is selected as 8 mm for better finish, symmetry  and prevent sharp edges.
7. Triangular grouts are positioned along the same base lines along the length as are the holes, grout thickness selected
randomly as 5 mm.
8. Grout angle is 45 degree for symmetric load distribution.
9. All the corners and edges along the bracket were run by a 0.5 mm chamfer to remve sharp edges and better aesthetics.
